META · DEVICE ACCESS TOOLKIT

From the chef’s eyes.
To your side of the board.

We use Meta’s Device Access Toolkit in the native Echo app to connect the glasses to an iPhone. The same first-person perspective captures the chef’s method and helps the learner follow it.

01 · CAPTURE

The chef wears them once.

Record the recipe while explaining what to do and why. Pinch to mark a new step without putting down the food.

02 · CONNECT

The iPhone runs Echo.

The native app connects camera, controls and supported lens display to the lesson. Pairing starts on the phone; the browser is for review, simulation and monitoring.

03 · FOLLOW

Keep your hands on the task.

Follow a short step card with the chef’s reference, request a check and receive a specific correction while cooking.

A real chef lesson has been recorded through the glasses and processed into a published recipe. The complete Cook flow on physical glasses still needs end-to-end validation.

01 · STRUCTURE

A recording becomes a lesson.

Extract timestamped steps, reference moments, success criteria and known mistakes from the chef’s video. The chef reviews the draft before publishing.

02 · CHECK

Compare the work.

Compare the learner’s camera frame with the current step and chef reference. Return Pass, a concrete Fix, or Can’t tell when the view is unclear.

03 · WATCH

Catch a known mistake.

The watcher looks for the lesson’s known mistakes between checks. Repeated confident detections trigger a short warning and spoken instruction.

04 · EXPLAIN

Ask about the method.

Gemini Live supports spoken questions using the lesson and the chef’s narration as context, so help stays connected to the recipe.

ELEVENLABS · TEXT TO SPEECH

A clear voice.
Without looking away.

We use ElevenLabs to prepare spoken lesson lines for the native app. Step instructions and familiar prompts can play from bundled recordings, keeping the voice consistent while your hands are busy.

Echo plays the prepared recording when one is available and falls back to iOS speech for missing or dynamic lines.
